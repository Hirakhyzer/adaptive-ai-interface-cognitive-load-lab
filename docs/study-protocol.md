# Study Protocol

This document outlines a possible controlled HCI study. It is not an approved ethics application.

## Study aim

Evaluate whether adaptive AI interface behaviour improves usability, perceived workload, task performance, trust, and user autonomy compared with a static interface.

## Participants

A future study could recruit adult participants with basic digital-task experience. Recruitment should follow institutional guidance and informed consent requirements.

## Procedure

1. Explain the study purpose and adaptation logic.
2. Collect consent.
3. Assign participants to static and adaptive task conditions.
4. Ask participants to complete synthetic digital tasks.
5. Collect task metrics and short questionnaires.
6. Conduct optional reflection questions.
7. Debrief participants and explain limitations.

## Research materials

- Task scenarios.
- Interface screenshots or prototypes.
- Workload and trust survey items.
- Interview or reflection prompts.
- Data management plan.

## Safeguards

- Use synthetic tasks.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
- Allow withdrawal.
- Avoid deception unless explicitly approved.
- Explain that adaptation is experimental and optional.
