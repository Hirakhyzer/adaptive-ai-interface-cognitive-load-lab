# Cognitive Load Signals

This project uses synthetic signals for controlled interface experiments. The signals are not personal measurements and should not be used to judge real users.

## Signals used in the prototype

| Signal | Prototype meaning |
|---|---|
| Response time | Longer task completion time may suggest more support is useful. |
| Hesitation count | More pauses may suggest uncertainty in the task. |
| Revision count | Repeated edits may suggest recovery support is useful. |
| Interruption count | More interruptions may suggest batching non-urgent prompts. |
| Help requests | Help-seeking may suggest clearer guidance is useful. |

## Boundary

The signals are for synthetic HCI research only. They are not medical, psychological, employment, or productivity assessments.
